A bibliography is more than just a list of sources at the end of a paper. It is a tool that demonstrates how deeply an author engaged with the topic and allows readers to verify the information independently.

## What Is a Bibliography and Why Does It Matter?

A bibliography is an ordered list of all sources used when writing a scientific or academic paper. It serves several functions:

- **Confirms credibility** — readers can verify every claim
- **Protects against plagiarism** — all borrowings are explicitly listed
- **Shows research depth** — demonstrates the volume of material the author worked through
- **Helps other researchers** — find related sources on the topic

## Main Citation Styles

Academic practice has several bibliography formatting standards. The choice depends on the discipline and institutional requirements.

### GOST R 7.0.5-2008

The Russian national standard, mandatory at most Russian universities. Example for a book:

```
Ivanov, A. V. Foundations of Scientific Research / A. V. Ivanov. — Moscow : Nauka, 2020. — 320 p.
```

### APA (American Psychological Association)

Used in psychology, sociology, and education:

```
Ivanov, A. V. (2020). Foundations of scientific research. Nauka.
```

### MLA (Modern Language Association)

Popular in the humanities:

```
Ivanov, Alexei V. Foundations of Scientific Research. Nauka, 2020.
```

## Types of Sources and Their Formatting

> [!NOTE]
> Different source types are formatted differently. It's important not to mix up the scheme for a book, an article, and a web resource.

**Book (monograph):**
Author, initials. *Title*. — City : Publisher, year. — Number of pages.

**Journal article:**
Author, initials. Article title // Journal Name. — year. — Vol., No. — pp. pages.

**Web resource:**
Author. Material title [Electronic resource]. — URL: address (accessed: dd.mm.yyyy).

## Reference Management Tools

Working with a large number of sources manually is inconvenient. There are dedicated reference manager programs:

| Tool | Free | Notes |
|------|------|-------|
| **Zotero** | ✅ | Best choice for most tasks, browser plugin |
| **Mendeley** | ✅ | Great for PDFs and collaborative work |
| **EndNote** | ❌ | Professional tool for serious researchers |
| **CiteMachine** | ✅ | Online citation generator, quick and simple |

## Common Mistakes

> [!WARNING]
> These errors appear in most student papers — better to check in advance.

1. **Source is in the text but not in the list** — or vice versa
2. **Inconsistent style** — some sources in GOST, some in APA
3. **No access date** for web resources
4. **Wrong order** — GOST requires alphabetical order
5. **Outdated sources** without justification — prefer publications from the last 5–10 years

## Summary

A well-formatted bibliography is a sign of respect for the reader and for scholarship. Take a little time to learn the required standard, use reference managers, and check your list before submission.
